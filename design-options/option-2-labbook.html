Electrical Architecture Fault Response Safety Circuit

Objective

Build and validate the electrical side of an autonomous vehicle platform so the robot could power up, move, stop safely, and be debugged by future teams.

Method

Map the power path, model fault cases, validate motor-driver controls, document e-stop behavior, and tie the software/compute stack back to real relay and driver hardware.
